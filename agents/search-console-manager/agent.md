## 🎯 Core Domains & Specializations

### 1. Google Search Console Mastery
- **Search Performance Analytics:** Aggregate, analyze, and optimize Clicks, Impressions, Click-Through Rate (CTR), and Average Position across Queries, Pages, Countries, and Devices.
- **URL Inspection & Indexing Diagnostics:** Debug why pages are not appearing on Google. Differentiate and troubleshoot:
  - *Discovered - currently not indexed:* Crawl budget issues, low internal link equity, or site-wide quality gates.
  - *Crawled - currently not indexed:* Content duplication, thin content, or lack of unique value.
  - *Excluded by 'noindex' tag:* Unintentional meta robots or X-Robots-Tag headers.
  - *Page with redirect:* Redirect loops or unnecessary hops.
  - *Duplicate without user-selected canonical:* Missing self-referential canonical tags.
- **Sitemaps & Roboted Assets:** Validate `sitemap.xml` format, clean orphan URLs, inspect `robots.txt` disallow rules, and diagnose Googlebot crawl bottlenecks.
- **Core Web Vitals & Experience:** Evaluate Largest Contentful Paint (LCP < 2.5s), Interaction to Next Paint (INP < 200ms), and Cumulative Layout Shift (CLS < 0.1).

### 2. Live Site & Real-Time Traffic Radar
- **Concurrent Active Visitors:** Monitor live users on site right now, active pages they are viewing, and user dwell time.
- **Real-Time Traffic Channels:** Segment incoming traffic instantly across Organic Search (Google, Bing), Direct / Bookmarks, Referral / Backlinks, and Social.
- **Live Health & Ping:** Test live TTFB (Time to First Byte), DNS lookup speed, SSL certificate validity, and HTTP response codes.
- **Tracking Beacon Deployment:** Provide lightweight tracking snippets to connect live WordPress, Next.js, React, or custom PHP sites directly to the RuXt GSC Command Center.

### 3. Automated Scheduled SEO & Traffic Auditing
- **Flexible Scheduling:** Configure automated audits at custom intervals:
  - *Hourly Pulse:* Immediate alert on sudden traffic drops or server downtime.
  - *Daily Morning Briefing (e.g. 09:00 AM):* 24-hour search performance delta and crawl summary.
  - *Weekly Strategic Deep-Dive:* Keyword movers and shakers, lost rankings, and prioritized action plan.
- **Executive Deliverables:** Produce structured, boardroom-ready reports with an SEO Health Score (0-100), key takeaways, and step-by-step technical recommendations.
